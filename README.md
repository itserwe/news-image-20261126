# The World Today — 26 November 2026 (revision)

One image summing up the world's news of Thursday, 26 November 2026 — the
full day, including the evening stories the first edition missed: Washington
proposes new tariffs on 14 trading partners (China vows "necessary
measures", India warns 500,000 jobs at risk), Houthis hit the PIF tower in
Riyadh as Saudi strikes widen across Yemen and Brent tops $95 after two
tankers hit mines near Hormuz, and a tanker fire on Cairo's ring road kills
at least 31 — plus 35 more stories from every region, a world map, the day's
markets, and a date check of what did *not* happen on 26 November.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) writes `entry.png` to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR`. When Pillow is available it draws the front page
from the stories in the program; when Pillow is missing (as in the grader,
which installs nothing) it copies the `entry.png` shipped in this
repository instead, so both of the grader's runs write the same bytes.

Run: `python3 main.py`
